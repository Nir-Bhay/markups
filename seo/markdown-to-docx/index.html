When to export markdown as DOCX

Use DOCX output when stakeholders need tracked changes, inline comments, and familiar review workflows in Word. This is common for legal drafts, proposals, policy docs, and executive review cycles.

Best-fit use cases

  • Client-facing documents requiring Word edits.
  • Cross-functional reviews with non-technical teams.
  • Formal handoff documents with multiple approvers.
  • Institutional workflows built around Office tools.

Export quality checklist

  • Use consistent heading levels for navigation.
  • Keep list indentation clean and predictable.
  • Avoid overloaded tables with too many columns.
  • Review spacing and section breaks after export.
