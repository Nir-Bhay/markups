Privacy Policy

Markups is designed so your editor content stays in your browser, while public pages may use limited analytics and diagnostics to understand traffic and keep the site stable.

Direct answer

We do not intentionally store your markdown drafts on our servers. The editor is browser-first, so your content remains in your device storage unless you export, share, or otherwise move it yourself. Public pages may still use analytics tools to measure visits and performance.

What we collect

  • Page views, device type, browser type, and similar usage signals on public pages.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address through analytics providers.
  • Any message you send through GitHub issues or the repository.

What we do not collect

  • Your draft markdown content as part of normal editing.
  • Account credentials, because the app does not require a login.
  • Private file uploads to a backend, because the core editor works locally in the browser.

Why we process data

Service operation

To deliver pages, protect the site from abuse, and keep the public experience working as expected.

Measurement and improvement

To understand which pages are useful, identify broken experiences, and improve the public site.

Third-party services

Public pages on Markups may load services such as Vercel hosting and analytics tooling, including Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Vercel Web Analytics, fonts, and GitHub links. Those providers may process technical data according to their own policies.

Your rights

  • Access the data we hold about you, if any.
  • Ask us to delete personal data that we control.
  • Object to or limit processing where the law allows it.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.
  • Use browser settings to clear local storage, cookies, and site data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the repository issues page: github.com/Nir-Bhay/markups/issues.
