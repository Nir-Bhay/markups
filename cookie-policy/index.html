Direct answer

The editor mainly relies on local browser storage such as IndexedDB or localStorage for settings and draft state. Public pages may use cookies or similar identifiers from analytics, hosting, and diagnostics tools.

Storage used by the app

  • Local browser storage for drafts, preferences, and UI state.
  • Session-based data needed to keep the app usable while you browse.
  • No server-side document database for normal editor use.

Third-party cookies or identifiers

  • Analytics tools that measure visits and engagement on public pages.
  • Diagnostics tools that help with performance and error tracking.
  • Provider-specific cookies and similar identifiers governed by those vendors' policies.

How to manage them

  • Clear browser cookies and site data in your browser settings.
  • Use browser privacy controls or extensions that limit tracking.
  • Block third-party cookies if that matches your preferred privacy level.

What this means for you

Blocking analytics cookies should not stop the editor from opening, but it may reduce measurement quality on public pages. Clearing site data can remove locally stored drafts and preferences in your browser.
