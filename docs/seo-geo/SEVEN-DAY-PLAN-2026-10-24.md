## Definition of useful progress

Observed rather than promised: Google-selected canonicals, indexed eligible URLs, nonbrand impressions/clicks, qualified editor starts, export completions, accepted independent directory links, mentions/citations in saved AI outputs. No numeric uplift target is evidence-based with the current small baseline. Track absolute changes and a 30-day follow-up; do not claim a one-week causal ranking win.
